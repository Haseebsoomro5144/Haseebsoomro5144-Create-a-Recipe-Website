Chicken Biryani

A classic rice dish with spicy chicken and fragrant spices.
Perfect for family dinners and parties.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg chicken
  • 3 cups basmati rice
  • 2 onions (sliced)
  • Yogurt, ginger garlic paste
  • Biryani masala

Method

  1. Marinate the chicken with yogurt and spices for 1 hour.
  2. Fry the onions until golden brown.
  3. Cook the chicken until tender.
  4. Boil the rice until 70% done.
  5. Layer rice over chicken and cook on low heat (dum) for 20 minutes.

Cooking time: 90 minutes* | Serves: 4

*Time can change depending on the stove.

Fluffy Pancakes

A quick breakfast that takes only 20 minutes.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup flour
  • 1 egg
  • 1 cup milk
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 1 tsp baking powder

Method

  1. Mix all the dry ingredients in a bowl.
  2. Add egg and milk, then whisk until smooth.
  3. Pour a ladle of batter on a hot pan.
  4. Flip when bubbles appear and cook the other side.

Toppings you can try

  • Fruits
    • Banana
    • Strawberry
  • Sweet
    • Honey
    • Chocolate syrup

Kitchen Words

Whisk
To mix quickly using a fork or whisk.
Dum
Cooking slowly on low heat with the lid closed.
